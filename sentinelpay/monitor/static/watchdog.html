How it works. Every transaction that was debited from the customer but not credited to the receiver gets a reversal deadline (default T+1 day for UPI-style transfers). Rows turn amber when less than 3 hours remain (3 minutes in demo mode) and red once the deadline passes, when compensation of ₹100 per day of delay accrues.

Deadlines and compensation amounts are configurable and based on RBI TAT guidelines for failed transactions; verify against current RBI circulars. This is not legal advice.
